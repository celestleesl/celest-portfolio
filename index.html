Case Study · Physio & Sole Clinic Ongoing since 2024

Making medical marketing feel human.

Healthcare marketing has to earn trust before it earns a click. Most people don't find a physiotherapy clinic by browsing — they find it mid-search, in pain, trying to figure out what's wrong before they've even decided to book an appointment. The content strategy starts from that moment, not from the clinic's services list.

I run the ongoing content and SEO strategy: keyword research to map what patients are actually typing into Google, on-page optimization so the clinic's pages genuinely answer those questions, and a content calendar that keeps output consistent instead of reactive.

Day to day, that means blog content built around specific conditions and symptoms (the kind of "why does my heel hurt when I run" queries people actually search), service pages rewritten around local SEO so the clinic surfaces for near-me searches, and a steady stream of educational posts across social to keep the account active between campaigns. I also track what's ranking and what isn't, and adjust the calendar around it rather than sticking to a plan that isn't working.

The work isn't only digital. I've also helped bring the brand into physical spaces — like the roadshow booth pictured above, promoting the clinic's foot care line to a walk-in crowd, with printed collateral and signage designed to match the same voice as the online content.

This sits alongside my day-to-day as Marketing Executive across Physio & Sole and its sister brand AnjouHealth: planning campaigns, running email and paid social, and reporting on what's actually moving the needle.

Case Study · AnjouHealth Ongoing

A confident, minimal wellness vibe.

AnjouHealth is Physio & Sole's sister brand, built around a calmer, more minimal take on workplace wellness. I manage its digital content as part of a multi-brand marketing team, which means keeping a distinct voice for Anjou while staying efficient running two brands at once.

The day-to-day covers social channels, blog posts, and website updates aimed at strengthening outreach and engagement — consistent enough to build a presence, without losing the calm, confident tone the brand is built on. That includes visual direction too: keeping the feed's palette and photography style minimal and considered, rather than defaulting to the busier, more promotional look a lot of healthcare marketing falls into.

I'm also on the ground for event support, translating workplace health and safety topics — which can get dense fast — into content that's clear and visually engaging rather than clinical. That's meant building simplified one-pagers and social recaps from technical WSH material, so the takeaways land with an audience that isn't reading it as a compliance document.

Because Anjou and Physio & Sole share a marketing team, a lot of the value is in the connective tissue — cross-promoting where it makes sense, reusing what works across both brands, and making sure Anjou never just reads as "Physio & Sole's sister site" rather than its own thing.

Case Study · Women & Children Centre Ongoing

Structured content for a sensitive subject.

Women & Children Centre covers subject matter that calls for a different kind of care than most content briefs. The goal is straightforward — grow organic search presence — but the execution can't be: every piece has to feel structured and trustworthy without ever reading as clinical or cold.

I manage the content and SEO strategy end-to-end: structured, search-intent-led planning that anticipates what someone is actually looking for and meets them there, with the clarity and sensitivity the subject deserves. In practice that means organizing content around the actual questions and concerns patients search for rather than a generic services list, building out pages with clear headings and structured FAQs so both readers and search engines can scan them quickly, and being deliberate about tone — reassuring and plain-spoken, never alarmist, never vague.

For a subject like this, trust signals matter as much as keywords: accurate, up-to-date information, clear sourcing, and a consistent editorial voice across every page, so the site reads as a place people can actually rely on rather than just another search result.
